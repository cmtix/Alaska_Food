# Variable Definitions for summary_group_market_dashboard.csv

## Dataset Overview
This dataset contains State of Alaska food procurement records from a distributor, showing monthly aggregated purchase data by market, product classification, and pricing information from April 2023 to December 2024.

## Variables

### Temporal Variable

**month_year** (date)
- Month and year of procurement record
- Format: YYYY-MM-DD (always first day of month)
- Range: 2023-04-01 to 2024-12-01
- Example values: "2023-04-01", "2024-12-01"

### Geographic Variable

**market_name** (string/categorical)
- Location of distributor warehouse where products originated; SEATTLE SOUTH serves the whole souteast, and ANCHORAGE serves the rest of the state. AK producers would have to sell to the distributor at the warehouse level.
- Example values: "ANCHORAGE", "SEATTLE SOUTH"
- Represents procurement/distribution centers serving Alaska

### Product Classification Variables (Hierarchical)

**pim_class_canon** (string/categorical)
- Top-level product classification (canonical class)
- Broadest product category
- Example values: "BEEF", "BEVERAGE", "DAIRY", "PRODUCE, FRESH", "SEAFOOD", "SPECIALTY MEATS"

**pim_category_description** (string/categorical)
- Mid-level product classification (category)
- More specific than class, broader than group
- Example values: 
  - "BEEF ROAST, CORNED BEEF & PASTRAMI"
  - "COFFEE"
  - "CHEESE, CHEDDAR"
  - "POTATOES, FRESH"

**pim_group_description** (string/categorical)
- Detailed product classification (group)
- Most specific product description level
- Includes preparation state, storage method, and specific varieties
- Example values:
  - "BEEF, GROUND, RAW, FROZEN"
  - "CHEESE, CHEDDAR, SLICES"
  - "POTATOES, WHOLE (WHITE), FRESH"
  - "COFFEE, ROAST AND GROUND CAFFEINATED"

### Quantity and Sales Variables

**total_net_wt_shipped** (float)
- Total net weight of product shipped in the period
- Unit: pounds (lbs)
- Example values: 72, 4160, 1521.99

**total_sales** (float)
- Total sales value for the product in the period
- Unit: US dollars ($)
- Example values: 585.02, 5196.88, 9195.41

**total_cases** (float)
- Total number of cases shipped
- Can include fractional cases (e.g., 0.25, 1.9999)
- Example values: 6, 104, 0.75, 2.25

**est_preoh_price_per_lb** (float)
- Estimated pre-overhead price per pound (negotiated case-level overhead rate netted out for calculation)
- Calculated as: total_sales / total_net_wt_shipped
- Unit: dollars per pound ($/lb)
- Example values: 7.94777777777778, 1.196, 26.1635379084967

## Data Structure
- Each row represents a unique combination of: Month/Year, Market, and Product (at the group level)
- Total observations: 20,755 rows
- Time period: April 2023 - December 2024
- Hierarchical product classification: Class → Category → Group

## Data Types Summary
- **Date**: month_year
- **String/Categorical**: market_name, pim_class_canon, pim_category_description, pim_group_description
- **Float**: total_net_wt_shipped, total_sales, total_cases, est_preoh_price_per_lb

## Notes
- The dataset represents aggregated State of Alaska monthly procurement data from a major in-state distributor
- Price per pound is estimated and excludes overhead costs
- Cases can be fractional, reflecting partial case purchases
- Product classifications follow a three-tier hierarchy (class > category > group)
